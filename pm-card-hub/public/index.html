GM CONTROL // ROUND FLOW

Round 1

Players can tap UNDO ATTACK on their used cards until you end the round. Ending the round discards the remaining queued player cards and used/targeted enemy cards, then each player draws from their pile. Unused hand cards stay.

Cards each player draws after ending a round: 1.

No rounds have ended yet.

Draw enemy cards

Enemy draws are unlimited. Choose an enemy type, then draw randomly here or use DRAW THIS CARD in the enemy roster to choose exactly which card to put into play. Drawing another copy never removes an existing one.

Enemy attacks

Select ATTACK WITH THIS on a revealed enemy card above. Then click ATTACK THIS CARD on either player's hand below. The attacking enemy card is queued for discard at round end; the targeted player's card remains. Targeted enemy cards are also queued until round end.

Card speeds

Optional. Set a speed (0–99) for an enemy card or a card in a player's hand, then use the checkbox to show or hide speeds on everyone's screen.
